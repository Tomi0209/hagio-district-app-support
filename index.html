Account & Login

Forgot your password?

On the login page, tap “Forgot password?”. You will receive a reset link by email (check spam too).

Account creation:

During registration, passwords are validated for minimum length, uppercase, number, and special character. You must also accept the privacy and legal notices before creating an account.

Using the App

Daily Reflection (“Today”)

Displays your daily impulse and progress for the currently active program. From here, you can also log out and add items to Favorites.

Dashboard

Shows all available fixed programs and allows you to start one. You can also configure up to four daily push reminders (feature under improvement – an info text is shown). You also have the ability to delete your account from here.

Workbook

Premium users have full access to the integrated PDF (German & English). If the PDF does not load correctly, a Reload button is available. You may also export the PDF and open it externally.

Favorites

You can add Favorites directly from the Today page. They can be removed on the Favorites page. After saving at least 10 Favorites, Premium users can create custom programs from them.

Program Generation

Free users can restart the same program after completion. Premium users can generate new programs after completing a program.

Language Switching

On every page, you can change the app language between German and English via the flag icon in the bottom-right corner.

Admin Page

An internal admin page allows adding sources for generating impulse texts. A demo source is included for testing.

FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions

I forgot my password. What can I do?

Use the “Forgot password” link on the login page.

The daily reflection is not loading.

Try refreshing the page or logging out and in again.

The workbook PDF is blank or not loading.

Use the Reload button or export the PDF.

Why can’t I create a new program?

New program generation is a Premium feature. Free users can only restart the same program.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from the Dashboard. All data is permanently removed.

Notifications don’t work.

You can set up to four reminders. The app includes a note informing users that the notification system is still being improved.
