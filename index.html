Account & Registration

Users create an account with email and password. During registration, the app checks password strength and requires two confirmations:

  • Your data is only used for account creation and app functionality.
  • The app is not therapeutic and does not replace professional care.

You can log out at any time from the “Today” page. Your account can also be permanently deleted through the Dashboard.

Using the App

Daily Reflection (“Today”)

  • Displays your daily impulse for reflection/action.
  • Shows basic progress information for the active program.
  • Allows adding items to Favorites (Premium only).
  • Includes the logout option.

Dashboard

  • Shows all available fixed programs and individual programs (the latter are Premium only).
  • Allows you to start a program.
  • You can set planned times for up to four daily push notifications (the feature is not yet implemented; the app informs users about this).
  • Includes the option to permanently delete your account.

Workbook

Premium users can access the integrated workbook PDF in German and English.

  • If the viewer does not load correctly, a Reload button is available.
  • You can also open or export the PDF externally.

Favorites

  • Premium users can add Favorites directly from the Today page.
  • Favorites can be removed from the Favorites page.
  • After saving at least 10 Favorites, Premium users can generate custom programs based on them.

Program Generation

The app offers three types of programs:

1. Fixed Programs (available for all users)

  • Randomly generated programs with a fixed duration of either 14 or 30 days.
  • Free users can start one fixed program at a time and—after completing it—can only restart the same program.
  • Premium users can start any fixed program and generate a new one after completion.

2. Individual Programs (Premium only)

  • Premium users can generate a personalized program by answering a short set of questions.
  • The resulting program is tailored to the user’s answers and focus areas.

3. Favorites-Based Custom Programs (Premium only)

  • After saving at least 10 Favorites, Premium users can generate programs based on their Favorite items.
  • Free users cannot create these programs.

Language Switching

You can switch the app language at any time. A flag icon appears in the top-right corner on every page, except on the language selection page itself, where the flag is displayed in the center. You can also open the language selection page through the flag icon located in the bottom-right menu.

FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a “Forgot Password” option?

Not yet. The app currently does not include a password recovery feature. If you lose access to your account, you may need to create a new one. While account recovery can be helpful for preserving saved progress and Favorites, the app stores only minimal data, and all features remain fully usable by creating a new account.

The daily impulse is not loading. What should I do?

Try logging out and back in. A weak internet connection may also cause delays.

The workbook is blank or not loading correctly.

Use the Reload button or export the PDF to open it in another app.

Why can’t I create a new program?

Free users can only restart the same fixed program after completing it. Premium users can generate new fixed, individual, or Favorites-based programs.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account through the Dashboard. All stored data will be permanently removed.

Notifications are not working.

You can set up to four reminder times in the Dashboard. However, notifications are not yet implemented — the app displays an information message.
